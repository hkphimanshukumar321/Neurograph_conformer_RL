# Ethics & Responsible Use

## Data Ethics
- All datasets used are publicly available with established IRB/ethics approvals
- No individual participant identification is possible from the preprocessed data
- ZuCo participants provided informed consent under URPP Dynamics of Healthy Aging, University of Zurich

## Limitations (Must be stated in paper)
1. **Small sample sizes**: 14–30 subjects per dataset; results may not generalize
2. **Lab conditions**: Data collected in controlled lab settings, not real-world
3. **Vocabulary constraint**: Classification is closed-vocabulary (4–11 classes)
4. **No clinical validation**: This is a proof-of-concept, NOT a medical device
5. **Cultural bias**: English and Chinese only; no representation of other languages

## Responsible Deployment Claims
- **Do NOT** claim this is a "mind reading" system
- **Do NOT** claim real-time deployability without clinical validation
- **DO** state the confidence threshold and abstention rate
- **DO** acknowledge the gap between lab EEG and consumer EEG

## Dual-Use Considerations
- Technology could potentially be misused for surveillance or involuntary decoding
- Paper should include a discussion of ethical safeguards
- Consent and agency of the EEG wearer must be paramount
